How the numbers are made

Each cohort has its own fitted equation for the expected diameter. Terms that dropped out in stepwise selection are absent.

Yp = a × bfemale × agex × heighty × weightz
Yc = measured ÷ Yp
z = (Yc − mean) ÷ SD

Yc is a percent-predicted value: 1.15 means 15% larger than expected. The normal range in millimetres is Yp multiplied by the published lower and upper limits.

Reference limits

  • Chinese and Italian: mean, SD and 95% limits of the corrected value from Table 5 (n = 910 each). The flag uses those limits, as the authors recommend.
  • Combined: the paper gives no reference values. This page uses the sex-specific validation-group mean and SD from Table 4, with limits at ±1.96 SD. Treat it as provisional.
  • "High-normal" and "low-normal" mean inside the published limits but beyond the 95th or 5th percentile. That label is this page's addition.

Where it does not apply

  • Derived in healthy, normotensive, non-obese adults aged 18 to 79 (BMI under 30). No athletes, no pregnancy.
  • Age is in every equation, so age-related widening is treated as normal. Fixed or height-only cutoffs will read older patients more strictly.
  • The annulus was measured leading edge to leading edge at end-diastole, not inner edge in mid-systole.
  • Only Han Chinese and Italian subjects were studied. Other populations are an extrapolation.
